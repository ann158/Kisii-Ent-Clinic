Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I see an ENT specialist?

Come in if you have ear pain or discharge, hearing loss, a blocked nose or sinus problems that last more than 10 days, frequent sore throats or tonsillitis, a hoarse voice for more than two weeks, difficulty swallowing, or nosebleeds that keep coming back.

Can I clean my ears with cotton buds?

It is best not to. Cotton buds and sharp objects can push wax deeper or injure the eardrum. If you feel blocked or have pain, we can remove the wax safely.

My child put something in their ear or nose. What should I do?

Stay calm and do not try to dig it out with fingers or tweezers, as this can push it further in. Bring your child to the clinic soon. If your child is choking or has trouble breathing, go to the nearest emergency facility immediately.

How do I stop a nosebleed at home?

Sit upright and lean slightly forward. Pinch the soft part of your nose firmly for 10 minutes without letting go. If the bleeding is heavy, follows an injury, or does not stop, seek urgent care.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We care for babies, children, adolescents and adults.
